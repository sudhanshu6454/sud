# Merchant & Network Outreach Pack

## Open assumptions (to be confirmed in discovery)

- Launch connector target: one approved affiliate network and one direct
  merchant (§3, §6). No merchant relationship is assumed to exist yet —
  nothing in this pack promises traffic or commissions before approvals.
- Operating entity, consumer brand and domain are not yet chosen (§17).
- Categories and commission terms below are the brief's starting positions;
  actual eligibility is set by each merchant's programme (§6 capability
  registry).

## What the platform is

A content-to-commerce discovery platform for the Indian market (INR, English
and Hindi). We convert entertainment and paparazzi-style content into
shoppable "looks": a viewer sees an approved product linked to a clip, taps a
tracked placement, lands on the look, and checks out **with the merchant**.
We own discovery, referral attribution, publisher operations and commission
reconciliation. We do not hold inventory, process checkout, or touch customer
funds.

## The traffic proposition

- **Owned pages** with large entertainment followings, plus **invited external
  publishers** (creators, niche pages) — each onboarded, identity-verified and
  property-scoped. Approval is per property and per programme, not blanket.
- **Transparent source-level reporting**: every click carries a publisher,
  property, placement and campaign reference you can see in reconciliation
  reports. No blended, unattributable traffic.
- **Returns-adjusted numbers**: we report on validated, returns-adjusted
  conversions and reconcile every statement to conversion and ledger totals by
  merchant and currency. What we invoice is what you accepted, net of
  reversals.
- **Editorial control**: human-reviewed product matching ("exact item" only
  with evidence; otherwise clearly labelled "similar style"), ASCI-compliant
  disclosure on every placement, and a campaign kill switch that can pause any
  failing or unauthorised component without losing history.

## What we need from you

1. **Programme approval** — acceptance into your affiliate programme for the
   platform's operating entity, covering the launch categories and IN
   territory.
2. **Subpublisher permission** — where our model applies (we receive your
   commission and share it with contracted publishers), we need written
   confirmation that subpublisher/subnetwork use and our publisher properties
   are permitted under your programme (§5 operating models).
3. **Feed / API access** — product feed or catalogue API with item-level data
   (price, stock, GTIN/brand+model where available), image reuse terms, and
   offer-refresh cadence.
4. **Tracking integration** — deeplink tool or API, your supported subID/click
   reference field, and confirmation of the attribution window, coupon rules,
   new-customer rules, and eligible-sale basis.
5. **Event definitions** — agreed definitions for sale, return, cancellation
   and any lead/install events, with fraud criteria and evidence requirements.
6. **Reconciliation & settlement** — transaction reports or postbacks with
   provider transaction/line IDs, reporting latency, validation delay,
   returns window, payout basis and schedule.
7. **Point of contact** — a named partnerships contact for approval,
   disputes, and pauses.

## Our compliance posture (what you can rely on)

- ASCI-compliant, clearly visible disclosure on every commercial placement —
  captions, short video, and landing pages.
- Approval scoped per publisher property and programme; unauthorised
  subdelegation, cookie stuffing, forced redirects and fabricated activity are
  contractually and technically prohibited (§5).
- No rewriting of other affiliates' tags, no cloaked destinations, no extra
  tracking hops where your programme forbids intermediary redirects — we use
  your compliant link format and state reduced attribution capability openly.
- Consumer data minimised: no email, phone or raw customer identifiers in
  tracking URLs; attribution is deterministic click-reference mapping, never
  fingerprinting.

## Launch categories (brief §3)

Fashion, footwear, bags, accessories, and selected lifestyle products that can
be credibly connected to entertainment content. Beauty claims, luxury
authenticity and regulated categories are separate approval paths — flagged
here so expectations are set early.
